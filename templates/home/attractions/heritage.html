Liliw Through the Ages

A journey through the milestones that shaped our town

1571

Foundation

Liliw was established as a town during the Spanish colonial period, named after the abundant "Dilidili" trees in the area.

1646

Church Construction

St. John the Baptist Church was built, becoming the spiritual center of the community and a testament to Spanish baroque architecture.

1896

Philippine Revolution

Liliw played a significant role in the fight for independence, with local heroes like Gat Tayaw leading the resistance.

1900s

Tsinelas Industry

The footwear industry flourished, establishing Liliw as the "Tsinelas Capital" of the Philippines.

Photography Tips

The golden hour (early morning or late afternoon) provides the best lighting for capturing the beauty of historic structures.

Guided Tours

Contact the tourism office for heritage walking tours with knowledgeable guides who share fascinating stories and local legends.

Respect Heritage

Please be respectful of religious sites, private properties, and historical artifacts. Help preserve these treasures for future generations.
