Best Time to Visit

November to May offers the best weather for outdoor activities. Waterfalls are most impressive during and after the rainy season (June-October).

Safety Tips

Always bring a local guide, wear proper footwear, stay hydrated, and check weather conditions before your trek.

Eco-Tourism

Help preserve Liliw's natural beauty by following Leave No Trace principles and supporting local conservation efforts.
