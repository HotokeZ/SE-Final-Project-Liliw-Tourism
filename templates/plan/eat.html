Dining Tips

Best Time to Visit

Most restaurants are busiest during lunch (12-2 PM) and dinner (6-8 PM). Visit during off-peak hours for a more relaxed experience. Street food stalls typically open in the late afternoon and evening.

Payment Methods

While larger restaurants accept cards, many local eateries and street food vendors prefer cash. It's best to have small bills ready. Some establishments now accept GCash and other e-wallets.

Local Etiquette

Don't be shy to eat with your hands, especially when enjoying traditional Filipino meals. Say "Salamat" (thank you) to show appreciation. Tipping is appreciated but not mandatory - 10% is customary in restaurants.
