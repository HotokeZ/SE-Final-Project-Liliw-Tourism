Writing Guidelines

  • Be respectful and honest in your review
  • Share practical information and helpful tips
  • Use clear and engaging language
  • Include specific details about locations and experiences
  • Upload high-quality, relevant photos
  • All submissions are subject to moderation before publishing

Writing Tips

  • Start with a hook: Grab readers' attention with an interesting opening
  • Be specific: Include names, locations, prices, and practical details
  • Use storytelling: Share personal anecdotes and experiences
  • Add value: Provide tips that help other travelers
  • Proofread: Check for spelling and grammar errors

Photo Guidelines

  • Use horizontal (landscape) orientation
  • Minimum resolution: 1200x800px
  • Good lighting and clear focus
  • Showcase the subject prominently
  • Avoid watermarks or logos
